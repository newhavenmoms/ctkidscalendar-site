Age Group: Adults
Program Type: Arts & Crafts

Event Details:
Come craft with us in the Maker Space. Meet other crafters at this in-person monthly tutorial. Learn what our equipment can do without having to know how to use any of it! Each craft features elements pre-made using one of the machines and is customizable. Each month uses a new piece of equipment.

As a bonus, you can ask our Maker Space experts questions about the space and equipment. Take a look at other projects and make a one-on-one appointment to have us walk you through it.

Perfect for beginners, ages 18 and up.

Add colorful fall napkins and ribbon to a foam pumpkin for a fall-tastic pumpkin. Finish it off with some fun lace or ribbon and you walk away with a cute fall staple!

Novel Ideas Book Club
2:00pm–3:00pm
Barney Library
Library Branch: Barney Library
Room: Hoppin Gallery
Age Group: Adults
Program Type: Discussion Groups
Join us for lively and thoughtful discussions on the last Tuesday of every month at 2:00 PM at the Barney Library, 71 Main Street. Discover something new each month as we explore a wide range of genres including fiction, mystery, literary fiction, nonfiction, and biographies. At the end of each meeting copies of the upcoming selection will be available to borrow, with additional copies available to check out from library’s service desk afterward. Everyone is welcome! No registration required. Even if you haven’t finished the book, feel free to attend (just be prepared for possible spoilers).
