    Swipe for rainy-day spots and short drives

    Get outside

    • Harvey's BeachA small, sandy town beach on Long Island Sound.
    • Saybrook Point & Fort Saybrook Monument ParkRiverside walking paths and history signs where the Connecticut River meets the Sound.

    Rainy day, indoors

    • Acton Public LibraryWeekly playgroup, storytime and Sing & Stomp, plus a children's room with toys.
    • The Kate (Katharine Hepburn Cultural Arts Center)A restored theater on Main Street with films, concerts and a free small museum about Katharine Hepburn.
    • Old Saybrook Historical SocietyKeeps the 18th-century General William Hart House; check open hours and events.

    Worth a short drive

    • Essex Steam Train & RiverboatVintage steam-train rides along the Connecticut River, including the holiday North Pole Express.
    • Connecticut River MuseumHands-on river exhibits in Essex, plus a holiday train show from late November.
    • Hammonasset Beach State ParkConnecticut's longest public beach, plus the Meigs Point Nature Center's touch tanks and trails, in Madison.
    • Toys Ahoy! (Essex)A classic village toy store on Main Street in Essex, with toys, games and children's books.

    Privacy policy

    Effective October 2, 2026

    Old Saybrook Kids Calendar (ctkidscalendar.com/old-saybrook) is part of CT Kids Calendar, a free guide to events, classes and resources for families across Connecticut. This site has no accounts. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, and it only sets cookies if you allow them. This policy explains what information changes hands when you use the site.

    Information we collect

    We don't ask for personal information. You can browse the calendar, classes and resources without telling us who you are. The site remembers your language, your cookie choice and any ZIP code you search with in your browser's local storage; those stay on your device.

    To learn which towns, events and features are useful, we use Google Analytics 4. If you choose “Allow” in the cookie notice, it sets analytics cookies and records the pages you view, the links and buttons you tap (like Share or Español), the website that sent you here, your approximate location (city or region) and your device, browser and screen size. Google Analytics doesn't store your IP address. If you choose “No thanks,” no analytics cookies are set, and Google receives only basic signals without cookies or identifiers, used for overall statistics.

    You can change your choice any time: Cookie settings. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google’s opt-out browser add-on.

    How we use it

    • To publish event details we're given, like the name, place, time, ages, price and the organizer's public link.
    • To talk with businesses and organizations about sponsorship.

    We don't sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

    Services we rely on

    A few outside services help run the site, and they may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects to them:

    • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may keep server logs, including visitor IP addresses, for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.
    • Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google. See Google's privacy policy.
    • Google Analytics measures how the site is used, as described above. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s privacy policy.

    The site also links to other websites, such as Google Maps, Google Calendar, event organizers and community resources. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

    Children's privacy

    This site is written for parents and caregivers. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. This site doesn't currently have a way to submit information directly, which further limits this risk.

    Keeping and deleting your information

    We don't currently have a public contact address, so no emails are being collected yet. This policy will be updated when that changes.

    Changes to this policy

    If we add something new, like a newsletter sign-up, we'll update this policy first and change the date at the top.
