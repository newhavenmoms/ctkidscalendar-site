    Swipe for rainy-day spots and short drives

    Get outside

    • Wickham ParkA big park on the East Hartford line with playgrounds, themed gardens, an aviary and trails (parking fee).
    • Oak Grove Nature Center53 acres of woods, a pond and trails, run by the Lutz Children's Museum.
    • Center Springs ParkA large town park near downtown with a pond, trails and sledding in winter.
    • Botticello FarmsA big farm stand and pumpkin patch on Hillstown Road, busy every fall weekend (209 Hillstown Rd).
    • Olcott Street SpraygroundA free sprayground open daily 10–8 from late May through Labor Day weekend (121 Olcott St).
    • Charter Oak Park and Union Pond Park skatingFree outdoor ice skating with lights when it's cold enough for safe ice.

    Rainy day, indoors

    • Lutz Children's MuseumA hands-on children's museum with a live-animal room. Open Tuesday–Sunday; $13–14 per child and adult (247 S Main St).
    • Manchester Public LibraryThe new downtown Main Library (opened Sept 2026, 1041 Main St) has a rooftop garden patio, a Friday-morning playgroup and storytimes most weekday mornings.
    • The Fire MuseumA 1901 wooden firehouse full of antique fire engines in the Cheney mill district (230 Pine St). Open Saturdays 10–4.
    • Old Manchester MuseumThe historical society's museum of town history, with a one-room-schoolhouse corner (126 Cedar St); call 860-647-9983 for hours.
    • Once Upon A ChildA kids' resale shop with gently used toys, books and gear (410 Middle Tpke W).
    • The Firestone Art Studio & CafeWalk-in pottery, canvas and wood-sign painting seven days a week, with a cafe (1115 Main St).
    • Lava IslandAn indoor play park with trampolines, foam pits, slides and a toddler area, open daily 9–9 (Fri–Sat to 10). Kids $16 an hour or $22 all day; adults $8 (169 Hale Rd).
    • Flight Adventure ParkA big trampoline and inflatable park with a Kidz Zone for under-4s and an arcade; grip socks required (145 Spencer St).
    • KidZoneInflatables, an arcade and a toddler area, Wednesday–Sunday (412B Middle Tpke W). Ages 3+ $18, 2 and under $13.
    • Stone Age Rock GymAn indoor climbing gym open daily, with a kids' climbing club (195 Adams St). Day pass about $15.
    • Barnes & Noble, Buckland HillsA big bookstore with a children's section at The Shoppes at Buckland Hills, where there's also a free indoor play area by the food court.

    Worth a short drive

    • Connecticut Science CenterTen floors of hands-on science, including KidSpace for kids 7 and under, in Hartford.
    • Dinosaur State ParkReal dinosaur tracks under a geodesic dome, plus trails, in Rocky Hill.
    • Bushnell ParkHartford's downtown park, with a 1914 carousel in the warmer months.

    Privacy policy

    Effective October 2, 2026

    Manchester Kids Calendar (ctkidscalendar.com/manchester) is part of CT Kids Calendar, a free guide to events, classes and resources for families across Connecticut. This site has no accounts. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, and it only sets cookies if you allow them. This policy explains what information changes hands when you use the site.

    Information we collect

    We don't ask for personal information. You can browse the calendar, classes and resources without telling us who you are. The site remembers your language, your cookie choice and any ZIP code you search with in your browser's local storage; those stay on your device.

    To learn which towns, events and features are useful, we use Google Analytics 4. If you choose “Allow” in the cookie notice, it sets analytics cookies and records the pages you view, the links and buttons you tap (like Share or Español), the website that sent you here, your approximate location (city or region) and your device, browser and screen size. Google Analytics doesn't store your IP address. If you choose “No thanks,” no analytics cookies are set, and Google receives only basic signals without cookies or identifiers, used for overall statistics.

    You can change your choice any time: Cookie settings. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google’s opt-out browser add-on.

    How we use it

    • To publish event details we're given, like the name, place, time, ages, price and the organizer's public link.
    • To talk with businesses and organizations about sponsorship.

    We don't sell, rent or trade your information, and we don't add you to a mailing list without asking.

    Services we rely on

    A few outside services help run the site, and they may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when your browser connects to them:

    • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may keep server logs, including visitor IP addresses, for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.
    • Google Fonts supplies the site's typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google. See Google's privacy policy.
    • Google Analytics measures how the site is used, as described above. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s privacy policy.

    The site also links to other websites, such as Google Maps, Google Calendar, event organizers and community resources. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

    Children's privacy

    This site is written for parents and caregivers. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. This site doesn't currently have a way to submit information directly, which further limits this risk.

    Keeping and deleting your information

    We don't currently have a public contact address, so no emails are being collected yet. This policy will be updated when that changes.

    Changes to this policy

    If we add something new, like a newsletter sign-up, we'll update this policy first and change the date at the top.
